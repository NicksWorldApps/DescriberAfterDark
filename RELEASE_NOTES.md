## What's new in 1.2.0

- **Choose your model.** Press Alt M in the main window, or choose Choose model from the tray icon's menu, to open the Models list. Arrow through it: each model says its name, whether it's downloaded or in use, its download size, and what it's good for. Press Enter to use one; if it isn't downloaded yet, it offers to download it first.
- **Eight uncensored vision models to pick from,** from a tiny 1.9 GB one for older computers up to a large, very detailed one for powerful graphics cards. The default stays the same.
- **Download and remove models from the list,** with progress spoken every 10 percent. Stopped downloads continue where they left off. Removing a model frees its disk space.
- **Add any other Ollama model by name,** for advanced users. Text-only models are flagged, since they can't see pictures.

## About Describer After Dark

- Press Control+Alt+D to describe the image or image link on your clipboard. Works with Luna for Reddit ("Copy post image link"), Reddit for Blind, web browsers, copied images, and image files.
- Speaks through NVDA, JAWS, and other screen readers, with follow-up questions about the picture.
- Runs entirely on your own computer through Ollama. No images are sent anywhere.

**To install:** download the `-win-x64.zip` file below, extract it, and run `DescriberAfterDark.exe`. If you have an older version, exit it from its system tray icon first and extract over it; your settings are kept. If you have version 1.1.0 or later, you'll be offered this update automatically. New users: on first launch it helps you install Ollama and download the description model (about 6 GB). Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
